Citizen Workflow

How It Works

Transparent 3-stage governance process keeping citizens informed from submission to resolution.

1

Submit Request

Citizen logs or registers the incident with category, precise address/location, description, and optional photo.

2

Officer Review & Dispatch

Department administrators inspect the case, triage priority, assign field personnel, and mark status to In Progress.

3

Resolution & Rating

Once field repairs are completed, status transitions to Resolved. Citizen inspects outcome and provides feedback.
